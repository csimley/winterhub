Middle school players (grades 6–8) take part in chess, futsal and the winter technical program. Strength + speed and indoor games are for high school players, so they’re hidden.

Which position block is mine?

Thursday technical sessions include a block for your position. Pick the channel where you play most.

  • Central channel: center backs, center mids, attacking mids and strikers. You play through the middle, so you practice receiving on the half-turn, playing forward, and holding the ball with your back to pressure.
  • Wide channel: wingers and outside backs. You play along the touchline, so you practice receiving down the line, beating the press, going 1v1 and crossing.

Play more than one spot? Pick the one Coach uses you at most. You can switch any time.
